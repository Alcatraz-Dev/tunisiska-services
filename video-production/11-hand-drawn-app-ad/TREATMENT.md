# Treatment — A Route Sketched by Hand

## Brief and product understanding

A 30-second vertical, screenshot-free paid ad for Tunisiska Mega, in Swedish and Arabic. The app serves the Sweden–Tunisia corridor with scheduled trips, parcel and container shipping, moving, moving with cleaning, and airport taxi. Travelers can manage bookings; senders can follow shipment status. Keep those factual features, and avoid dated examples, prices, delivery promises or invented availability. The supplied logo appears only on the final card.

The app identity carries through the illustration: navy `#132B55` and deep navy `#0B1B33`, warm orange `#F2761B`, soft warm-neutral surfaces, rounded shapes, and Rubik for compact supporting labels. Large headings are handwritten; sketched sepia outlines and paper texture soften the vector motifs. Arabic copy is set right-to-left with readable Arabic fallback shaping.

## Three structures

1. **One line, many services (chosen).** A hand draws the Sweden–Tunisia route; the moving orange line becomes transport, parcel, home, taxi, booking and tracking sketches, then resolves under the app mark. This gives a 30-second ad one continuous, memorable gesture.
2. **Sketchbook pages.** Six service illustrations are drawn on separate warm-paper pages that turn into one another. Clear and charming, but repeated page turns interrupt the route and compress the call to action.
3. **One parcel's journey.** Follow a parcel through pickup, freight, tracking and arrival. Intimate, but it would make the broader service catalogue feel secondary.

The route-led structure best preserves the earlier ad's service breadth while making the drawing itself the hook and transition.

## Logline and arc

A hand-drawn orange route travels between Sweden and Tunisia, sketching the services that help people and parcels make the journey, then becomes the invitation to explore the app.

**Opening:** sepia pen finds a route on blank cold-press paper. **Turn:** the route branches into practical services, then becomes a booking/tracking trail. **Ending:** the line loops back into the brand mark and CTA, echoing the first stroke.

## Benchmarks

- **Urban-sketch sketchbook convention:** learn the fine imperfect sepia line, transparent misregistered washes, warm paper, and drawing-as-action. Do not borrow any scene, props, shot, text, or layout from the style demo.
- **The Red Balloon (1956):** learn to let one colored object guide the camera through a place. Do not borrow its balloon, characters, compositions, or plot.

## Visual and motion system

The medium is pen-and-wash: a warm paper field; thin, visibly imperfect sepia ink; translucent navy and orange watercolor-like blooms; white paper reserves; hand-lettered titles; readable Rubik labels. Keep navy and orange the only strong pigments so the app remains recognizable. Hand outlines wobble subtly and do not close perfectly. The six transport/service drawings are original code-built shapes; no screenshots, screen recordings or interface images.

Each main icon draws in before its wash blooms. The route moves with a smooth camera follow; no elastic easing. A restrained 112 BPM plucked pulse and brush-like paper ticks follow the pen. No voiceover; localized on-art headings and separate SRT captions.

## Sound and delivery

Original instrumental score, no voice. Target −14 LUFS with true peak below −1 dBTP. Deliver Swedish and Arabic MP4s, localized SRTs, posters, project source, treatment and credits. Canvas 1080×1920 at 24 fps, approx. 30 seconds.

## Claims and assets

Service claims match the reviewed app source. The route is symbolic, not a geographic map. No prices, schedules, guarantees, fabricated status or testimonials. The user-supplied logo is the only image asset; all transport/service artwork is drawn in code. Bundled Rubik is OFL. System fallback lettering is used where available; no external typeface is redistributed.

# Treatment — Tunisiska Mega: One route, many services

## Brief

- **Purpose:** 30-second vertical paid-social app ad.
- **Versions:** Swedish and Arabic, with separate title copy and captions. Swedish uses the provided StoreReady screenshots; Arabic uses the earlier Arabic simulator captures where the in-app language matters.
- **Format:** 1080×1920, 24 fps, H.264 MP4. One 30-second master per language.
- **Voice:** None. Continue the text-led approach from the previous ads, with an original, beat-led score and dry procedural foley.
- **CTA:** “Utforska appen” / “استكشف التطبيق”. No unverified app-store link or contact details.
- **Material:** Tunisiska Mega logo; provided store-screen kit; existing Arabic safe UI crops; custom code-drawn grid and route line.

## Three possible structures

1. **One line, one route — chosen.** A single orange rule enters an empty paper grid. It becomes a route, links the real service screen to trip information and live-status UI, then resolves into the end-card rule. It gives the feature sequence a clear visual spine and a Swiss-style native move.
2. **The service index.** The service-list screenshot breaks into a grid of individual categories, which reassemble as one app screen. This is clear but gives less room to explain trip and status features.
3. **Zoom ladder.** Start inside one service row, widen to the trip route, then pull out to a set of app screens. This offers scale but makes the route less memorable.

I chose the first structure because one route line can connect the service, planning and status screens without implying that any particular departure is current.

## Logline and arc

**Logline:** One orange route line links the many steps of a Sweden–Tunisia journey to the services and trip information in Tunisiska Mega.

**Arc:** An open question (“Resa eller frakt?”) becomes a clear set of service choices; the line then leads through route information and trip status, before closing as a simple invitation to explore the app.

## Benchmarks

Do not borrow its custom “Heidi Maria” typeface, wordmark or layouts. [Swiss Films’ identity note](https://www.swissfilms.ch/en/news/swiss-films-with-a-new-look/5887)
- **Apple’s “Don’t Blink” product film:** Learn the economy of a strong beat and one clear message per cut; keep this film slower so every service label is readable. Do not borrow Apple copy, graphics, marks or music. [Film overview](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Don-t-blink-The-Apple-September-iPhone-7-event-in-a-107-second-video.173669.0.html)

Apple’s guidance supports adapting app marketing assets for promotion; this work uses Tunisiska Mega’s own interface and logo. [Apple marketing resources](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/promote/)

## Visual system

Use the **Swiss Motion Graphics** rules: warm white paper, black/charcoal type, light greys and one orange signal colour. Adapt the style’s 12-column landscape grid to an 8-column portrait grid with 48 px side margins and a 24 px baseline. Keep the screens flat and square to the grid: no device mockup, gradient, shadow, grain, glow or bounce. Moves are precise eased snaps, clip reveals, wipes and knife cuts. One orange line is the protagonist; it draws across the layout, aligns with the app’s route visuals, and becomes the end-card underline.

Use the supplied app screens as contained artifacts. Mask exact departure dates, clock times, capacity counts, speed/distance figures and notification text in the store screenshots so the ad remains evergreen and does not publish trip or message content. Exclude profile, wallet, admin and empty-state screens.

## Cue map

- **Tempo:** 120 BPM; 24 fps, two frames per tenth-second; section edges on half-second beats.
- **Bars:** six 5-second sections; an additive score adds one instrument layer per section, then subtracts back to a single mallet pulse for the ending.
- **Visual hits:** screen panels and headlines land on beats; the orange line is drawn continuously only in the opening and the signature transition.
- **Silence:** near-zero mix from 13.5–14.0 s before live status, and 24.5–25.0 s before the end card.
- **Voice:** none. All spoken-caption checks are omitted; on-screen copy is held for legibility and a separate Swedish/Arabic SRT accompanies each master.

## Sound design

| Section | Bed | Foley / hit |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | One low, dry motorik pulse | Pencil/ruling-pen stroke; felt thump as the line stops |
| Services | Add a restrained marimba-like two-note pattern | Paper slide and short press clacks, one per emphasized row |
| Route | Thin the score; keep the pulse | Pen line sweep, then dry label snaps |
| Live status | One-beat near-silence, then pulse returns | Soft status tick and paper panel landing |
| Three-panel system | Add light shaker subdivisions | Three distinct print clacks, spaced on the grid |
| End card | Remove percussion; single sustained tone | Silence before logo; one soft stamp on the final beat |

The score and foley will be synthesized for this film; no third-party music or sample pack is planned.

## Type and captions

Use an OFL sans-serif face and one weight family per language variant. Swedish is flush-left, ragged-right; Arabic is right-aligned with correct RTL shaping. Headlines sit in the grid, not a bottom subtitle bar. Avoid tight tracking on Arabic. Maintain the same orange as the single signal colour. Supply burned-in titles plus separate SRT caption files.

- Route and live-status visuals are custom code-drawn graphics; no dated trip screen or sample values are shown.
- Logo: `assets/logos/tunisiska-mega-original.png` from the earlier package.

- The route line is an abstract graphic, not a geographic map; it does not assert a precise route or border.

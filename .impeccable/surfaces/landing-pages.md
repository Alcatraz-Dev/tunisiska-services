# Landing pages

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Start with the Sweden–Tunisia trip route, then make all app services easy to discover. Refuse a generic headline-and-grid page with no booking path.

**OWN-WORLD:** Carry the app's Rubik typography, navy primary, orange action color, pale zinc canvas, white surfaces, soft borders, and compact route/capacity diagrams into a responsive web layout.

**STORY:** Visitors understand the route and departure flow first, then see parcel shipping, container shipping, moving, moving and cleaning, airport taxi, and shipment tracking. Booking and current availability continue in the app.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact navigation bar uses the actual Tunisiska Mega logo. A route-first headline sits beside a polished phone preview built from the current app style and the real configured app icon. Apple App Store and Google Play download badges are prominent, with departure browsing as the secondary route into the product. The matching trips and services pages repeat the download actions.

**FORM:** Responsive multi-page marketing set: home, trips and departures, and all services. The user selected route-first. Concept seed: 824dbb46 (degraded roll; no external challengers). Extend only existing product facts; no invented schedules, prices, testimonials, or performance claims.

**FINISH:** Keep the landing page design in the Kombai canvas for this phase; the separate web project is not part of this deliverable. Use the real in-app logo and configured app icon. Link the Apple badge to the verified App Store listing and Google Play to the URL based on the configured Android package ID; confirm the Play listing before launch. The landing-page canvases remain design prototypes until separately implemented.

# Treatment — Tunisiska Mega: One Route, More Possibilities

## Brief and audience

A 30-second vertical paid ad for people who travel, send goods, or move between Sweden and Tunisia. Deliver Swedish and Arabic versions. Continue the earlier screenshot-free requirement: no app screens, UI reproductions, or captured footage. The supplied logo and the app's bundled Rubik font are allowed brand assets; every scene and service illustration is drawn in code.

The app's product scope is scheduled trips, parcel/container freight, moving, move-and-cleaning, airport taxi, booking management, and shipment tracking. We use concise service labels and make no claims about prices, schedules, speed, reliability or availability.

## Three concepts

1. **One route, then a launch reveal (chosen).** An orange stroke slashes across a deep navy field between Sweden and Tunisia. It accelerates through fragments of transport symbols, strikes a timed reveal at 9 seconds, then transforms into a sequence of service illustrations and a restrained brand close. This gives the audience an immediate visual hook and a simple narrative thread.
2. **Service dominoes.** A parcel knocks over a sequence of service symbols that flow from one to another. Playful, but it tilts the product toward shipping and needs more visual setup.
3. **A moving type poster.** Large words, custom letter masks and a single line build the brand in a designed typographic sequence. Strong and minimal, but the service range is less concrete.

The route-led launch gives the ad a clear opening, single 9-second brand beat, fast readable benefits and clean CTA.

## Logline and arc

A bright route connects Sweden and Tunisia, gathers momentum into the Tunisiska Mega reveal, then becomes the path through the services that help people travel, send and move.

- **Hook (0–3):** The route cuts through darkness and reveals the two destinations. No logo.
- **Tease (3–9):** Brief glimpses of trip, parcel and vehicle pictograms build toward the reveal; the same orange path moves consistently rightward.
- **Reveal (9–13):** The supplied brand mark and name snap in on the exact 9.000-second drop, scale 1.16→1.0, then settle with no competing motion.
- **Feature run (13–25.5):** Five full-frame shots, one benefit each, on 2.5-second beat intervals. A single bold word plus one original vector illustration per shot. The repeated type entrance and wipe create a coherent rhythm.
- **End card (25.5–30):** Logo, one short line and one CTA remain still for at least 2.5 seconds.

## Sound first: tempo and cues

Original synthesized score, composed before animation at 120 BPM, 4/4. Beat = 0.5 s; bar = 2 s. The reveal drops at 9.000 s on a measured beat. Feature cuts land at 13, 15.5, 18, 20.5 and 23 seconds. The end card lands at 25.5 seconds. A rising noise sweep leads into the 9s drop; a short low impact and chord change mark the reveal, not a louder stack of effects. The beat strips back at 25.5 seconds and resolves with a small sonic tag.

## Visual language

High-contrast editorial motion graphics, deliberately different from the previous paper-and-card concepts. Deep navy and near-black fields alternate with warm off-white flashes; app orange is the single kinetic signal. Oversized Rubik typography occupies a clear safe zone. Original vector silhouettes use confident asymmetry, cropped scale changes, off-center framing, and short parallax depth. No UI screens, nested panels, paper texture, doodles, or rows of floating feature cards. Rounded app surfaces appear only where necessary to keep a real icon recognizable; no mock screen is recreated.

The orange route is the through-line and transition device: it connects the opening nodes, forms objects, underlines labels, and closes behind the end-card brand. One entrance curve and one fast exit rhythm carry across the feature shots; framing changes between close, medium and wide to avoid a flat icon carousel.

## Delivery and checks

- Two H.264/AAC masters: 1080×1920, 24 fps, 30 seconds.
- Swedish and Arabic SRT/VTT captions and rendered posters.
- Editable canvas source, timeline, score, treatment and asset notes.
- Inspect a 2 fps contact sheet and key frames at the hook, brand reveal, montage cuts and CTA. Check RTL shaping, safe areas, text collision, route continuity, decode, silence/black frames, loudness and cut-to-beat alignment.

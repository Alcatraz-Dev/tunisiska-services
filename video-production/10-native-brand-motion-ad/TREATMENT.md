# Treatment — One App, the Sweden–Tunisia Route

## Brief

- **Purpose:** Full 30-second paid-social app advertisement, made as original motion graphics.
- **Language versions:** Swedish and Arabic, separate localized masters and captions.
- **Canvas:** Vertical 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, H.264/AAC.
- **Picture:** Code-drawn illustration, routes, service pictograms, and app-native cards. No app screenshots or screen recordings.
- **Identity:** Use the actual Tunisiska Mega navy, orange, neutral palette, Rubik weights, rounded card shapes, and restrained elevation from `app/theme/colors.ts`, `typography.ts`, and `spacing.ts`. Use the user-supplied brand mark on the end card.
- **Story:** Show how one Sweden–Tunisia route connects several practical services in one app.
- **Voice:** None. Original, picture-synchronized synthetic score and dry tactile foley.
- **CTA:** “Utforska appen” / “استكشف التطبيق”. No store URL, phone number, price, schedule or unsupported guarantee.

## Product understanding

The reviewed app presents Tunisiska Mega Transport as a service app for the Sweden–Tunisia corridor. Its current catalogue covers scheduled trips, parcel shipping, container shipping, moving, moving with cleaning, and airport taxi. Its trip tools include route/departure details, booking management, shipment tracking and live status. The app's visual system uses a navy-led hero, warm orange for emphasis and actions, pale neutral backgrounds, bold Rubik headings, rounded white cards, and controlled soft elevation. Its own transport fallback artwork is vector-drawn (truck, van, ship, plane), so this ad can use the same illustrative logic without reproducing an app screen.

The provided screens contain dated schedules and example capacity/route values. Those are useful for understanding the product but are not ad artwork; none will be reproduced as screenshots or as specific live values.

## Three candidate structures

1. **One route, many services — chosen.** An orange route path crosses a dark navy hero and changes into a series of illustrated service actions. Each action becomes one branded card, then the cards assemble into an abstract app hub and end card. This mirrors the product's route story and makes the service range easy to scan.
2. **A day of logistics.** A parcel moves from pickup to delivery, with transport, storage and taxi services appearing as steps. This would tell one clear story but under-represent scheduled trips and the breadth of the app.
3. **A catalogue of six icons.** One service per fast pictogram card, then a six-icon wall. This is easy to understand but has less emotional continuity and could feel like a feature checklist.

The first option gives the app a memorable visual spine while keeping every claim tied to an actual feature.

## Logline and arc

**Logline:** One connected route links travel, parcel and container freight, moving services, and airport taxis inside Tunisiska Mega.

**Arc:** A line opens the Sweden–Tunisia corridor; the route becomes a vehicle and then a chain of service icons; the chain resolves into a concise app-native service hub and invitation to explore.

## Visual system

Use a **brand-native vector motion style** informed by the installed Pictogram Motion skill. Retain its readable object-first pictograms, modular sequencing, precise 24 fps timing, and type masks. Follow the app's actual tokens: navy `#132B55` and deeper `#0B1B33` for hero fields, orange `#F2761B` for one active signal, canvas `#F4F6FA`, white cards, neutral blue-grey supporting text, Rubik type, a 4-point spacing rhythm, and 20–28 px-equivalent corner radii. Use the app's own soft navy gradient for hero transitions and its restrained card shadow/elevation. Orange stays on route, active icon and CTA; no extra decorative accent colours.

All scenes are drawn from primitives and paths. Service pictograms are original code-built silhouettes that follow the app's vector transport art: truck, container ship, moving box/house, cleaning tool, airport taxi, route and calendar. Cards resemble the app's rounded neutral cards, but they do not reproduce any particular screen layout, values, or screenshot. Use the provided logo only as the brand mark on the final card.

Motion follows the app's motion tokens: 170–560 ms emphasized/standard ease, decelerated entrances, quick fades only for secondary transitions, clean card rises and route drawing, no elastic bounce or handheld shake. Pictograms stay visually simple, with labels kept in separate text zones.

## Sound and timeline

Use one original 112 BPM pulse with muted, warm percussion and light plucked notes. Paper-soft clicks and low clacks follow the service pictograms. The route is a short airy sweep; the taxi lands on one warm bell-like note. Strip the beat for a half-second before the final brand mark, then resolve with a short logo chime. Target about −14 LUFS and at most −1.2 dBTP.

All main actions land on half-second beats. Hold each service title long enough to read; keep captions in a clear lower safe area and mirror the on-screen localized copy in separate SRT files.

## Claim and asset notes

- All listed services and trip/shipment tools are supported by the reviewed app source and the user's materials.
- Do not show the screenshot files in either video. Do not invent departure dates, prices, routes beyond the supported Sweden–Tunisia corridor, customer quotes, capacity, delivery guarantees or operational performance.
- The only imported visual asset is the user's Tunisiska Mega logo. Fonts are the project's already bundled Rubik files with their OFL license.
- The final route is symbolic rather than a geographic map. No border or unverified road geography appears.

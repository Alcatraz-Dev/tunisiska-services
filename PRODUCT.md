# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People arranging transport, shipment, container shipping, moving, cleaning, or airport taxi services through the Tunisiska app. The landing page should present the full set of services currently available in the app.

## Product Purpose

Tunisiska Mega helps customers discover services, book scheduled transport and other services, manage bookings, and follow shipments. The app supports travel and delivery between Sweden and Tunisia.

## Positioning

No distinctive market-positioning claim has been confirmed. Keep landing page copy factual and based on capabilities visible in the app.

## Operating Context

Customers use an iOS or Android app to browse departures, book services, manage bookings, and track shipments. The requested landing page designs are a web surface that will later guide a separate landing page project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- App services currently include scheduled trips, parcel shipping, container shipping, moving, moving and cleaning, and airport taxi.
- The app includes trip capacity and route details, booking management, and shipment tracking.
- The landing page must reflect the app's existing service set and current visual identity.
- No pricing, customer testimonials, service guarantees, or performance metrics were supplied for landing page use.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the Tunisiska Mega name and existing logo.
- Preserve the Sweden–Tunisia service corridor represented in the app.
- Match the current app's navy, orange, soft neutral, and crisp card-based visual language, as requested by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing app screens and design variants are in `.kombai/canvas/tunisiska-redesign.canvas`.
- The service catalogue is defined in `app/(tabs)/services.tsx`.
- The app's trip, booking, and tracking routes are in the `app/` directory.
- No approved customer quotes, reviews, published prices, or quantified results were found in the checked app content.

## Product Principles

- Make the available services easy to compare.
- Make the next booking step clear.
- Show route and shipment information in plain language.
- Use only product claims supported by the app or supplied materials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support English, Swedish, and Arabic copy. Arabic text should display in Arabic while the app's established layout remains left-to-right.
